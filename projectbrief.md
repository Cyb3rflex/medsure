Project Brief 



Basic Build - Problem:
In the suburbs of Northern Nigeria, the local residents of Ajala town would often complain of stomach upset after taking a locally recommended, sub-standard, medication by their so-called Chemist. 
Down in the riverine areas, a popular city has recorded over 18,000 cases of allergy from drugs. These have led to unchangeable mutations in the lives of some people, due to consistent intake of those medications without knowing they contained the ingredient of their allergy.
There was another case of over-dose to a 19 year old girl, who just finished examinations at a famous university in Ondo state. She never intended to kill herself. Due to sheer ignorance, she couldn't help but be overwhelmed by the desire to see her migraine leave.
Finally, in the southern part of the nation, there was a popular case of a king who lost his life due to the wrong combinations of medications when he battled diabetes.

All these cases have one thing in common; what was meant to be a cure to their pain led to more pain or even worse, their demise. Why? Ignorance.


Solution:
The Solution is Medsure. It is simply an application that confirms the quality of a product, explains the medicine's use, dosage , and warnings in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, or Igbo.
This is just to make sure that what you are taking to help you is beneficial and not harmful to you.
Built in the app is a pharmacist section, for flagged conditions.

How it is used:
1. Input. The User uploads a photo or types the medicine name.
2. The AI model extracts the text of the image.
3. The app checks the text against our curated MEDICINES database. 
It checks for expiry date, NAFDAC pattern, and INTERACTION_RULES.
4. If Red Flags exist: the app stops, shows warnings and escalates to the Pharmacist Queue.
5. If the pharmacist confirms the drug, it is passed to a dataset facts to Gemini to generate a simple, translated explanation.
6. Output. Display text and play the voice note.
